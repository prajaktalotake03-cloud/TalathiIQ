Prelims Syllabus Scheme

The Combined Group-C Prelims Exam is of 100 Marks comprising 100 objective questions with 1-hour duration. There is a negative marking of 1/4th (25%) for incorrect responses.

View Full Prelims Syllabus

Mains Syllabus Scheme

The Mains Exam consists of 2 Papers (200 marks each, 400 marks total). Paper 1 covers Marathi & English (100 Qs), while Paper 2 covers General Studies & Reasoning (100 Qs).
