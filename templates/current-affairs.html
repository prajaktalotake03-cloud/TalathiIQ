Maharashtra August 15, 2026

Maharashtra launches 'Pramod Mahajan Skill Development Scheme' phase-II

Under this phase, the government aims to establish skill centers in more than 500 rural blocks to empower rural youth with employment and self-employment training. This scheme targets the training of over 2 lakh candidates annually with active integration of technology partners.

India August 14, 2026

ISRO launches new earth observation satellite EOS-09

The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) successfully injected the Earth Observation Satellite (EOS-09) into its orbit using the SSLV-D4 launcher from Sriharikota. The satellite will provide high-resolution imagery for agricultural monitoring, forestry, hydrology, and disaster management.

Gov Schemes August 12, 2026

CM Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana Updates: Over 1.5 Crore Applications Approved

The Maharashtra state government announced that more than 1.5 crore eligible women have successfully registered under the Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana. Beneficiaries will receive a direct benefit transfer of 1,500 rupees per month. The verification process has been fully digitized to speed up approvals.

Awards August 10, 2026

Lata Mangeshkar Award 2026 Announced

The Maharashtra state government has declared the prestigious Lata Mangeshkar Award for Lifetime Achievement in music to a veteran classical vocalist. The award consists of a cash prize of 5 lakh rupees, a citation, and a memento to be presented at a cultural event in Mumbai next month.

Sports August 08, 2026

National Games 2026 to be hosted in Maharashtra

The Indian Olympic Association has officially confirmed that the upcoming National Games will take place across Mumbai, Pune, and Nagpur. The state is developing sports infrastructures to match global athletic standards, expecting participation from over 10,000 athletes.

Important Days August 09, 2026

August Kranti Din observed on 9th August

The nation observed August Kranti Din, commemorating the anniversary of the Quit India Movement launched in 1942 under Mahatma Gandhi. Tributes were paid to freedom fighters at Kranti Maidan (Gowalia Tank) in Mumbai, where the historic resolution was passed.

International August 05, 2026

UN Climate Summit 2026 sets new net-zero targets

World leaders gathered in Geneva for the COP31 preparatory assembly to outline tighter emissions thresholds. Emerging economies requested financial assistance and green tech sharing models from developed countries to meet their sustainability timelines.

Economy August 01, 2026

RBI monetary policy maintains repo rate at 6.50%

The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) of the Reserve Bank of India decided to keep the policy repo rate unchanged. RBI Governor cited retail inflation stability and robust GDP projection for the quarter as the primary factors for the status quo.
